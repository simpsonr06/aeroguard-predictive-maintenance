## 3. Structural Flow & Section Hierarchy

The scientific sections adapt standard IJSART headings to the specific research requirements:

1. **Title & Author Block** (with clearly labeled author placeholder: `[Author Name 1]`, `[Affiliation / Department]`)
2. **Abstract** (Exact verified experimental results: baseline metrics, degradation shifts, detection evasion, overhead)
3. **Keywords** (Predictive Maintenance, Machine Learning Security, Telemetry Manipulation, C-MAPSS, Artifact Authentication, Covariate Drift)
4. **I. Introduction** (Aviation maintenance context, ML adoption in prognostics, ground-station advisory role, research questions, contributions)
5. **II. Related Work & Research Gap** (NASA C-MAPSS prognostics, ML integrity & signing, adversarial time-series manipulation, drift detection bounds, cybersecurity frameworks)
6. **III. System Architecture & Threat Model** (Ground-side telemetry ingestion, threat actors, attack vectors, trust boundaries, STRIDE classification)
7. **IV. Methodology & Experimental Design** (FD001 dataset, dual evaluation protocols, causal temporal feature engineering, synthetic manipulation taxonomy, detection mechanisms)
8. **V. Experimental Results & Resilience Evaluation** (Baseline reproduction, artifact tampering experiments, single-sensor bias & ramp outcomes, multi-sensor & replay attacks, drift detection limitations, API hardening, operational latency overhead)
9. **VI. Discussion & Threats to Validity** (Asymmetric penalty compounding, the stealth dilemma, operational drift vs physical damage, internal/external validity, aviation safety boundaries)
10. **VII. Conclusion & Future Work** (Summary of answers to research questions, implications for ground-side PM systems, future physical bus defense research)
11. **References** (Fully verified academic and technical citations)
12. **Declarations & Acknowledgments** (Conflict of interest statement, data availability statement, code reproduction repository reference)
